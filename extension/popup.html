What this can’t tell you
Who opened it
Every recipient’s pixel travels inside the same message body, so one person opening the mail fires all of them. In testing, two recipients’ events landed 0.08 seconds apart.
Where they were
A request routed through a mail proxy reports the proxy, not the reader. A message read on a phone in Korea recorded as The Dalles, Oregon.
